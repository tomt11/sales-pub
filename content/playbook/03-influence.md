# Influence (New & Expanded) — Robert B. Cialdini
## Distilled playbook (frameworks in own words, applied to agri-finance origination)

> Role in the system: the PSYCHOLOGY underneath both trust and SPIN. Seven levers of
> automatic compliance ("click-run" responses). Two uses: (1) ethically deploy where they
> genuinely align with the client's interest; (2) RECOGNISE when they're being used on you
> (e.g. incumbent banks, counterparties). Ethics rule baked into all drills: use a lever only
> when it points at a truth — never manufacture it.

---

## 1. Reciprocation — give first, and the obligation to repay follows
- People feel compelled to repay favours, gifts, concessions — even uninvited ones.
- **Reject-then-retreat**: a concession from a larger ask triggers a reciprocal concession.
- *Agri use: lead every referrer relationship with given value — a sector benchmark, a rate
  review at no obligation, an intro to a useful contact. This is also Ferrazzi's generosity
  rule and Maister's "help first" — three books converge here.*
- *Recognition: a "free" bank health-check that obligates the client; calibrate your own gifts
  so they're genuinely unconditional or they raise Self-orientation.*

## 2. Liking — we say yes to people we like
- Drivers: similarity, compliments, cooperation toward joint goals, familiarity/contact,
  association with positives. Physical attractiveness halo (less actionable, still real).
- *Agri use: genuine common ground — fishing, kids, the family farm background Tom actually
  has. Similarity must be real; farming families have finely-tuned authenticity radars.*
- *Cooperation framing: "us vs the problem" (you + family vs the structure issue), never
  "me vs incumbent bank".*

## 3. Social proof — we follow what similar others do
- Most powerful under UNCERTAINTY and SIMILARITY of the reference group.
- *Agri use: "three cotton families on the Downs restructured this way last season" beats any
  brochure. Case stories must match the client's region/commodity/scale — similarity is the
  active ingredient. Build a (de-identified) case-story library in the app.*
- *Recognition: "everyone's fixing rates right now" — herd pressure isn't analysis.*

## 4. Authority — deference to credible experts
- Symbols: titles, credentials, dress, being introduced by others.
- **Key technique: credibility transfer** — an introduction from a trusted accountant confers
  more authority than self-claims ever can. This is the entire referral-network thesis.
- **Trustworthy authority hack: argue against your own interest occasionally** ("honestly,
  your current facility is fine for another year — call me when the lease comes up") —
  enormous credibility purchase, and it lowers Maister's S simultaneously.

## 5. Scarcity — opportunities seem more valuable when less available
- Loss framing beats gain framing (loss aversion). Deadlines and limited windows drive action.
- *Agri use (ethical only): REAL deadlines — rate-lock windows, pre-approval expiries, seasonal
  settlement timing, carry-forward super caps. Frame genuine costs of delay as losses:
  "waiting past March costs you the winter-crop planting window."*
- *Never manufacture scarcity — one discovered fake deadline ends a referrer relationship.*

## 6. Commitment & Consistency — we act in line with what we've already said/done
- Small, active, public, voluntary commitments grow into large consistent behaviour.
- *Agri use: get micro-commitments in sequence — "send the financials" → "meet the accountant"
  → "table at family meeting". Each advance (SPIN) is also a consistency anchor. Written
  beats verbal: confirm agreed next steps by email in THEIR words.*
- *Need-payoff answers are self-generated commitments — the client who has SAID why it's
  valuable stays consistent with their own statement.*

## 7. Unity — the "we" relationship; shared identity
- Beyond liking: people say yes to those they consider OF THEM — family, place, tribe,
  acting-together. Strongest with kin and co-created experience.
- *Agri use: Tom's genuine identity assets — ag-broking family background, Queensland,
  the rural community. "I grew up around this" is a unity claim, usable because true.*
- *Co-creation: build the solution WITH the family at the table (asking their advice,
  drafting the structure together) — co-creation produces unity and ownership.*
- *The Tiwi fishing trip is a unity-builder par excellence: shared experience → "we".*

---

## Defence module (recognising levers used on you / your clients)
For each principle, drills should include a "spot the lever" variant:
incumbent bank retention offers (scarcity + loss framing), "valued customer for 20 years"
(consistency exploitation), glossy bank authority symbols, manufactured urgency on rate locks.
Teaching clients to see these = giving value (reciprocity) + credibility (authority) at once.
