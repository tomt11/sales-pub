## Core principles

1. **Build the network BEFORE you need it.** Trust takes time; asking cold is buying the
   life-vest after the ship is sinking. Marathon, not sprint.
   *Tom's cadence-tracked CRM is exactly this — the app should score "touches without an ask".*

2. **Generosity and loyalty are the engine.** Never ask "how can they help me?" — ask
   "how can I help them?" Relationships are muscles, not cake: they strengthen with use,
   they don't deplete. (= Cialdini reciprocity, Maister help-first. Triple convergence.)
   *Concrete agri currency: client referrals BACK to accountants, market intel, sector
   benchmarks, intros across the network, invites (Tiwi trips).*

3. **Relationship glues.** Contacts become relationships through shared experience and
   quality time outside formal settings — sport, food, fishing, family. People are more
   real when they're having fun.
   *Tom's natural glues: fishing, go-karting, food. The app should track which glue each
   key contact shares and prompt glue-based touches, not just email cadence.*

4. **Don't make small talk — be genuinely open.** Share something real or interesting;
   be remembered. Have something to say (informed on many topics) and stand for something
   (a unique message / expertise).
   *Tom's unique message: "the agri lending specialist who grew up in ag broking and
   actually understands family succession" — every interaction should reinforce it.*

5. **Super-connectors.** A few people hold disproportionate, diverse networks (the Milgram
   small-world finding). Identify and invest in them deliberately.
   *Agri equivalents: senior turnaround partners, rural accountants with big books, livestock
   agents, rural journalists, industry-body figures. CRM should have a super-connector flag
   and a higher-touch cadence tier.*

6. **Blue flame + Networking Action Plan (NAP).**
   - Blue flame = intersection of passion and ability (Tom's: relationship-driven agri finance —
     already found; the app states it on the dashboard as the "why").
   - NAP = 3-year goal → 1-year → 90-day interim goals, each with: people to meet, places to
     be, tools to acquire. A-goal and B-goal per horizon.
   *App hook: NAP lives as the goals layer above the CRM; 90-day goals generate weekly
   networking tasks (e.g. "meet one new super-connector candidate this fortnight").*

7. **Personal brand.** Two-sentence branding message; packaging (how you show up, materials,
   digital presence); then your own PR — visible generosity, taking on what others won't.
   *Origination version: the LinkedIn presence, the sector notes you circulate, how the
   Tiwi trip is followed up.*

8. **Mentors and peer group.** Your trajectory follows the people around you. Engineer the
   peer group: senior originators, top brokers, successful operators.
   *App hook: NAP should include a "who do I learn from" slot per 90-day cycle.*

9. **Sociable persistence.** Top networkers create contact volume daily and remember names
   (Clinton's cue-cards = a CRM discipline, decades early). Systematise memory: log personal
   detail immediately after every interaction.
